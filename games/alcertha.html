Alcertha

Development Disclaimer: I am a solo developer doing this as a hobby, so updates and development will most likely be slow

Alcertha is an open-world sci-fi/fantasy RPG about hunting monsters born from a magical catastrophe known as the "Flood." Build your own playstyle with a deep, unrestricted skill tree, recruit unique companions — each with their own full skill tree and personality — and take on a party of up to three as you explore a world where magic and technology have fused into "magitech." Research and dissect monsters to fuel your gear, uncover a world-spanning mystery, and decide how you want to hunt: lethal, tactical, or somewhere in between.
