About

Goncz Gaming is a solo hobby project — I'm the only developer working on everything here, from design to code to art. This isn't a company or a business; I do this alongside a full-time job, in whatever time I can carve out for it.

I make games because I love making games. Progress happens when it happens — sometimes quickly, sometimes slowly — but every project here is something I'm genuinely building for the love of it.
